FORWARD EVER, BACKWARD NEVER

Give a child in Dandora a stronger start

Your support helps keep Wits Garden's doors open to children whose families could never afford a premium private school — and helps a whole community move forward.

Why it matters

What your support makes possible

Dandora Phase 5 is a marginalized urban community, and for many families here, quality education can feel out of reach. Wits Garden exists to close that gap — and every gift, big or small, helps us do it a little better.

Affordable, quality education

By keeping fees within reach, Wits Garden ensures children from vulnerable backgrounds still get an empowering, quality foundation — not a watered-down one.

A safe, structured childhood

In a densely populated urban environment, a safe space is not optional. Your support helps keep children in a protective, structured setting each day, building essential social skills alongside academics.

Future leaders who give back

True to our motto, "Forward ever, backward never," we exist to unlock latent talent and help break the cycle of poverty — so today's learners become tomorrow's community leaders.

Our story so far

A school already at work in Dandora

Wits Garden already serves learners from Play Group through Grade 8, right here in Dandora Phase 5 — the classroom moments and everyday routines you see across this site are real, not staged. What we're asking for isn't to start something new, but to help what's already working reach further and hold steady.

With more support, that means more learning materials, better-maintained facilities, and a more secure future for the children already walking through our doors every day.

Questions

Frequently asked

How exactly will my donation be used?

Support goes toward the areas learners need most at the time — typically learning materials, meals and clean water, facility upkeep, or teacher support. Contact us directly for a current, specific breakdown.

Can I visit the school before donating or partnering?

Absolutely. Call or WhatsApp us to arrange a visit — seeing the classrooms and meeting the team is the best way to know exactly what your support goes toward.

Do you offer receipts or acknowledgment for larger gifts and partnerships?

Yes — reach out and we'll discuss what documentation or acknowledgment works for your gift or partnership.

Can I sponsor a specific learner?

Get in touch to talk through sponsorship options — we'll help you find the arrangement that fits what you have in mind.
